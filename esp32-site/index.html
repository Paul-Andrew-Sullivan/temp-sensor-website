Display buttons on the box

Pressing these does the same as pressing the buttons on the box. A sensor whose button is off is not shown on the box's own screen, but is still measured and graphed here.

Last 300 seconds

The graph moves left once a second. Missing data is hatched. A reading beyond the graph's range is drawn as a red line pinned to the top or bottom edge.

Email or text when it goes out of range

The message goes out once when a sensor crosses a limit, and again only after it has come back inside the range. For a text, use your carrier's email-to-SMS address, for example 3195551234@txt.att.net.
